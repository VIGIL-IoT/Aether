# Safety and regulatory status

## Current software

The browser simulator does not transmit or receive RF. It visualizes a deterministic software model and static frequency references.

## Hardware and firmware

Carrier R1 is an unbuilt design proposal. The legacy firmware has not been reviewed for deployment and includes transmitter-capable routines. No regulatory assessment, certification, RF exposure assessment, or operational safety review has been performed for this repository.

Do not infer legal permission, compliance, safe operating power, or safe separation distance from this project. Anyone considering transmitter-capable hardware is responsible for obtaining qualified review and consulting the current official requirements for the relevant jurisdiction and use. The repository does not provide instructions for RF operation.

## Battery status

Carrier R1 has no battery or charging circuit. Older battery-safety and charger descriptions in the repository do not apply to R1 and are withdrawn.

## Responsible project use

- Keep simulator results clearly identified as simulated.
- Do not present unvalidated firmware or the carrier proposal as tested hardware.
- Do not use transmitter-capable code to disrupt communications or operate equipment without appropriate authorization.
- Report security or safety concerns through the private reporting route in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

This document records project status; it is not legal, regulatory, or engineering approval.
