## Research method and limits

Crawl4AI 0.9.5 was used to retrieve the Espressif guide and official GitHub Action documentation. A Crawl4AI request to the W3C quick reference was blocked by its edge protection, so the normative WCAG page was consulted through a separate browser search. The Nordic specification PDF was also consulted directly after browser extraction failed. No scraped page content was copied into the repository.

The research supports design constraints and verification choices. It does not establish a working radio assembly, electrical margin, RF behavior, standards compliance, or the exact revision served by Vercel. See [hardware references](hardware-references.md) and the [engineering evidence ledger](engineering-evidence.md).
