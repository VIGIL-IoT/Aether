# Sustainable Development Goals assessment

Aether may be useful as a teaching aid, but the repository contains no course deployment, learner evaluation, accessibility study, or other outcome evidence. It therefore does not claim a demonstrated contribution to a United Nations Sustainable Development Goal.

SDG 4 (Quality Education) is a possible future context because the browser model presents a narrow channel-selection concept. That potential relationship is not evidence of educational impact. A defensible future claim would need a defined learning objective, a documented teaching setting, an evaluation method, and reported results with limitations.

No other SDG alignment is currently supported by the project evidence.
