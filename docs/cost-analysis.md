# Cost status

The repository does not contain verified current prices or a fabrication quote. The carrier part list is in [hardware/kicad/aether-carrier-r1/BOM.csv](../hardware/kicad/aether-carrier-r1/BOM.csv), but it lacks manufacturer part numbers, qualified suppliers, and pricing.

The former dollar totals and volume projections described obsolete or hypothetical designs. They are withdrawn and must not be used for purchasing, budgeting, or product pricing.

A useful cost estimate can be prepared after the exact mating DevKit and radio modules are selected and fit-checked. It should record supplier, part number, currency, quote date, quantity, shipping, taxes, board fabrication, assembly, and any enclosure or test costs.
