# Hardware bill of materials

The current carrier proposal's machine-readable part list is [hardware/kicad/aether-carrier-r1/BOM.csv](kicad/aether-carrier-r1/BOM.csv). It lists the carrier PCB, socket footprints, local capacitors, and the external mating boards.

This is not a procurement-ready BOM: exact manufacturer part numbers, prices, suppliers, and verified mating-part fit have not been established. Do not use older parts lists in this repository; they describe a battery-powered board that is not the current R1 design.
